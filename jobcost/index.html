Money is rounded to 2 decimals and percentages to 1 decimal. The price needed is rounded up to the next cent. Margin shows — when the price is 0; markup shows — when the cost is 0.

Margin vs markup

Both use the same profit, but divide by different numbers. People mix them up and end up underpricing.

Profit = Price − Total cost
Margin % = Profit ÷ Price × 100
Markup % = Profit ÷ Total cost × 100
Price for target margin = Total cost ÷ (1 − target)

Example: a job costs 1,000 and you charge 1,250. Profit is 250. Markup is 25% (250 ÷ 1,000), but margin is only 20% (250 ÷ 1,250). Adding 25% to your costs does not give you a 25% margin. For a 25% margin you need 1,000 ÷ 0.75 = 1,333.33… — the calculator rounds this up to 1,333.34 so you never land below your target.
