Data & privacy

Current status

Nothing you enter is sent anywhere. The check runs entirely in your browser.

Your selected industry, tasks and setup (no personal information) are used in aggregate to improve our tools. Free-text and names are never sent. Data is sent once when results are shown (the same result is not sent twice). The demo (?demo=) never sends.

What is sent (when collection is on)

  • Country (US, UK, CA, AU or OTHER) and industry
  • IDs of the tasks you picked, with how often and minutes (e.g. invoice:monthly:120)
  • Your setup answers (spreadsheet app, Windows/Mac, Microsoft 365, coding, budget tier)
  • IDs of the recommended methods
  • The date (day only, no time), the app version and the app name (dx-en)

What is never sent

  • Anything you type in "Other tasks" (it is only matched in this page and is not saved)
  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, company names or any contact details
  • Your action plan ticks

Stored on this device

Your answers and plan ticks are kept in this browser's localStorage so you can come back later. Clearing site data removes them. The check works even if storage is blocked.

How it is enforced

The page uses a Content-Security-Policy that blocks every connection except the receiver (Google Apps Script: script.google.com). No ads, external scripts or fonts are used. Received rows are stored in a Google Sheets spreadsheet managed by MisaNeco Works and used only for aggregate statistics.
